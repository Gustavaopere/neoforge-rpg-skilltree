# Universal Material Chemistry — Arcane Signature Policy

Arcane signatures exist to give fictional materials a rich visual identity without pretending they have real chemistry.

## Allowed

- project-original rune/sigil graphs;
- provider-lore-derived symbols when provenance and reuse are permitted;
- provider-visual-derived motifs when provenance and reuse are permitted;
- deterministic layout from curated signature data;
- explicit labels such as `Assinatura Arcana`.

## Forbidden

- fake element symbols;
- pseudo-IUPAC names presented as scientific;
- invented molecular formulae;
- claims that a historical rune has a specific occult meaning unless intentionally sourced and adopted into lore;
- automatic random/hash assignment presented as meaningful lore.

Hybrid materials may show proven real constituents alongside a separate arcane signature, never as a combined fake chemical formula.
